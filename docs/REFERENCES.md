# Canonical references and attribution

Reviewed 2026-09-19.

- Devoxx Robot Games reference library: https://game.devoxx.be/references.html
  - Voxxy: orange rounded head, bear-ear protrusions, dark visor, long orange/white articulated arms.
  - Droid: tall graphite humanoid, exposed joints and warm eyes.
  - Biggy: spherical blue-grey armour, weathered orange belly, squat legs and heavy arms.
  - Kinepolis venue: exhibition floor below auditorium level, connected by grand staircase, cinema corridors and seating/stage areas.
- Reachy Mini: https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini and https://store.pollen-robotics.com/collections/reachy-mini
  - White rounded head, two camera-like eyes, two antennas, small round base. No limbs or wheels.
- Microduck: https://pollen-robotics.com/microduck/
  - White shell, central camera, coloured hinged beak, exposed neck/leg mechanisms and biped feet. Reference behaviours include kick, grab, roller skating, and stand-up recovery.

All in-game geometry was authored procedurally for this project. Reference photographs/model sheets are not redistributed in the game. Venue proportions are deliberately compressed into readable combat slices. No official logos, third-party audio, or motion files are bundled. Names identify the source inspirations; this independent fan game is not endorsed by Devoxx, Kinepolis or Pollen Robotics.
